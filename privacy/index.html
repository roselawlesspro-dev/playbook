Privacy Policy

Rose Lawless (Instagram @iamroisinlawless) · App: reel-factory-insights · Last updated 13 November 2026

Who this covers

This policy explains how the reel-factory-insights app handles information. Rose Lawless runs the app for her own Instagram professional account, @iamroisinlawless. It is not offered to other businesses or the public.

What the app does

  • Reads performance statistics (views, reach, likes, shares, saves, watch time) for posts on @iamroisinlawless.
  • Publishes posts to @iamroisinlawless that Rose has created and approved.
  • Reads comments left on @iamroisinlawless posts. When someone comments a keyword the post asks for (for example "VIDEO"), the app sends that person one direct message with the link they asked for.

Information we collect

When you comment a keyword on an @iamroisinlawless post, the app stores your Instagram username, the comment's ID, the comment text and its date, along with a note of which message was sent to you and when. This prevents you from getting the same message twice. Comments without a keyword are read only to check for one, and are not stored.

We do not collect passwords, payment details, contact lists, location or any information from outside Instagram.

How it is used and shared

This information is used only to send you the message you asked for and to keep a record of what was sent. It is not sold, rented, shared with advertisers or used to build profiles. Some messages contain a referral link (for example to HeyGen). If you click it, that company's own privacy policy applies to whatever you do on their site.

Where it is kept and for how long

Records are kept in a private file on Rose's own computer, and deleted 12 months after the message was sent.

Deleting your data

To have your record deleted, send a direct message to @iamroisinlawless on Instagram saying "Delete my data". Your record will be removed within 30 days and you will get a reply when it's done. You can also remove the app's access at any time by deleting your comment or blocking the account.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change with it.

Contact

Send a direct message to @iamroisinlawless on Instagram.
